About SoarJMI

SoarJMI is a multidisciplinary student society at Jamia Millia Islamia. It brings together people with different interests and gives them a space to experiment, collaborate and turn their ideas into something tangible.

What We Aim For

To build a space where technology, creative expression and the culture around us can come together naturally.

What We Value

Curiosity, collaboration, honesty and the willingness to keep improving with every project and event.
